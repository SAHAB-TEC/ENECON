About This Module

The Complete Construction ERP for Odoo

A comprehensive construction project management module built natively for Odoo 18. Manage your entire project lifecycle — from planning and budgeting through procurement, execution, quality control, subcontracting, and invoicing — all within a single unified interface.

Whether you manage residential buildings, commercial complexes, infrastructure, or renovations, this module gives project managers, contractors, and finance teams complete real-time visibility into costs, materials, subcontractors, billing, and quality compliance.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects can I manage?

Any construction project — residential, commercial, infrastructure, renovations, and more. Supports hierarchical projects with sub-projects and phases.

How does budget management work?

Create budgets from BOQ with planned vs actual cost tracking, variance analysis, progress percentages, and multi-stage approval workflows.

Can I manage subcontractors?

Yes. Full subcontracting with scope of work, material consume orders, RA billings, completion certificates, and payment tracking.

Compatible with CE & Enterprise?

Yes. Fully compatible with Odoo 18 Community and Enterprise editions.

How does material requisition work?

Multi-stage approval workflow that automatically creates purchase orders and internal stock transfers once approved.
